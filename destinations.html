Six places that define a journey through Sri Lanka — each one woven into our tour packages.

Central Province

Sigiriya

UNESCO World HeritageAncient FrescoesWater Gardens

Rising 200 metres from the surrounding plains, this 5th-century rock fortress was once the citadel of King Kasyapa. Climb past the mirror wall and lion's paw gateway to frescoed galleries and royal water gardens engineered a millennium and a half ago.

Ella & The Highlands

Tea PlantationsHiking trailsScenic trains

A misty hill-country refuge where time slows down. Famous for the Nine Arch Bridge and endless expanses of emerald tea terraces, Ella is the heart of Sri Lanka's high-altitude adventure and colonial legacy.

Southern Province

Mirissa

Blue WhalesSurfingNightlife

A crescent-shaped cove that serves as one of the world's greatest staging posts for observing blue whales. Mirissa blends laid-back surf culture with secluded stretches of pristine golden sand and luxury beachfront villas.

View Related Tours →

Kandy

Sacred RelicsRoyal Botanical GardensCultural Hub

The last capital of the ancient kings' era. Set on a plateau surrounded by mountains, Kandy is home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, making it the most significant spiritual heartland for Buddhists worldwide.

Uva / Southern Province

Yala National Park

Leopard SafariSloth BearsGlamping

The most visited and second largest national park in Sri Lanka, boasting the highest leopard density in the world. Yala is a vast expanse of forest, grassland and lagoons offering unparalleled wildlife sightings.

View Related Tours →

Galle Fort

Dutch ArchitectureBoutique ShoppingSunsets

A living UNESCO World Heritage site. This 16th-century walled city jutting out into the ocean is a labyrinth of cobblestone streets lined with Dutch-colonial buildings, boutique cafes, art galleries and restored luxury manors.
